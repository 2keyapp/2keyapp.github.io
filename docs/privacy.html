Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 May 2026

This document is provided for general information only and is not legal advice. Consult qualified counsel for your situation.

2key.app (“we,” “us”) operates a hosted billing platform for software vendors. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you visit our website, sign up as a merchant, or interact with services powered by 2key.app.

Who this policy covers

  • Merchants — businesses that use 2key.app to sell subscriptions and deliver license keys.
  • Merchant customers — paying parties and using parties who purchase or use software billed through a merchant’s 2key tenant.
  • Website visitors — anyone who browses www.2key.app or related marketing pages.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Merchant signup: business name, email, and optional contact name.
  • Contact requests: email and message content when you reach us.
  • Merchant account and catalog data you configure in the platform (products, pricing, tax settings).

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and approximate access times (see our Cookie Policy).
  • Transaction and billing metadata processed through the platform (subscription status, invoice amounts, payment outcomes).

Information from payment providers

When you pay the platform setup fee or when payments flow through integrated gateways, Stripe and PayPal process payment data under their own policies. We receive limited confirmation data (e.g. payment succeeded, payer email where provided).

How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and improve the billing platform and website.
  • Provision merchant tenants, process subscriptions, seats, license keys, and entitlements.
  • Calculate tax, generate invoices, and meet billing obligations described in our Terms of Service.
  • Respond to support requests and send service-related notices.
  • Comply with law, prevent fraud, and enforce our policies.

How we share information

  • Service providers — hosting, email, analytics, and payment processors that help us run the service.
  • Merchants and their customers — billing relationships are between merchants and their customers; merchants control much of the data in their tenant.
  • Integrations — such as Earnfluence.io when merchants enable affiliate features.
  • Legal requirements — when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell personal information for money.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. Merchants may request deletion subject to applicable law and legitimate business needs (e.g. completed transaction records).

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International transfers

We may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at hello@2key.app to exercise these rights. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Privacy questions: hello@2key.app
