Cookie Policy

Last updated: 22 May 2026

This document is provided for general information only and is not legal advice.

This Cookie Policy explains how 2key.app (“we”) uses cookies and similar technologies on www.2key.app and related marketing pages. For how we handle personal data generally, see our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels.

How we use cookies

Strictly necessary

These are required for basic site operation and security. They may include preferences set when you use signup or navigation features. The marketing site itself uses minimal first-party storage.

Analytics (if enabled)

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand traffic and improve the website. If enabled, analytics providers may set their own cookies subject to their policies.

Payment providers

When you click through to Stripe or PayPal to pay the setup fee, those sites use cookies under their own cookie policies.

Merchant storefronts

Merchants operating on *.t.2key.app or custom domains may use additional cookies in their checkout flows. Those are controlled by the merchant and their configured gateways.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some site features may not work correctly if you block necessary cookies.
  • Where required by law, we will request consent before non-essential cookies.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send “Do Not Track” signals. There is no uniform industry standard; we review practices as standards evolve.

Updates

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
