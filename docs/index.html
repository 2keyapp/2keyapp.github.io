License keys and billing

Sell subscriptions. Deliver license keys and activation.

2key means to get the license key—like classic software activation, but for modern SaaS. 2key.app is hosted billing with seats, anniversary plans, storefronts per merchant, and client SDKs that deliver entitlements to apps—starting with Flutter.

How it works

Separate who pays from who uses. One organization can buy seats and assign each seat to any using party—employees, customers, or departments.

Paying vs using parties

A paying party (e.g. your customer’s company) subscribes once; many using parties consume the product under that subscription.

Seat-based licensing

Subscriptions are purchased as seats. Reassign seats as teams change without buying duplicate subscriptions.

Anniversary billing

Each paying party has one anniversary date; monthly and annual plans renew on that calendar. Onboarding and proration explains new purchases.

License keys and entitlements

2key means to get the license key. After purchase, using parties receive activation data your app understands—online from the billing server or offline via a signed JWT.

From purchase to activation

A paying party buys seats; you assign using parties. Each seat ties subscription state to someone who can run your software.

Online entitlements

Client SDKs and server APIs fetch current plans, features, and seat assignments while the app is connected.

Offline license keys

Using parties copy a signed JWT from the merchant web portal into the app—ideal for air-gapped or intermittently connected environments. See SDKs.

Taxes

2key billing calculates tax from merchant rules and customer billing addresses—at checkout and on every recurring invoice—with a clear breakdown customers expect.

Calculation and jurisdiction

Tax runs at checkout and on subscription renewals, one-time charges, and prorations. Rates follow the paying party’s billing address and your configured rules per jurisdiction.

Merchant tax settings

Define rates per region, choose tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive catalog pricing, and add tax registration IDs (VAT, GST, EIN, etc.) for invoice footers.

Invoices and compliance

Each invoice stores jurisdiction and rate snapshots so historical documents stay explainable. Credit notes reference original tax lines on adjustments.

Subscription lifecycle

Recurring billing does not stop at the first payment. 2key manages renewals, plan changes, and revenue growth motions through the subscription lifetime.

Renewals and plan changes

Renewals on the paying party’s anniversary. New purchases are prorated to that date; upgrades and downgrades prorate mid-cycle.

Upsell and cross-sell

Offer add-ons and complementary plans at checkout or during the relationship— orchestrated on 2key after Stripe or PayPal authorizes payment.

Credit notes

Adjustments and reversals reference original invoice and tax lines so finance teams can audit what changed. Details in Taxes.

One-time and hybrid

Subscription-first, with support for one-time charges alongside recurring seats—licenses, setup fees, or consumable add-ons.

Onboarding and anniversary alignment

Each paying party has one anniversary date. New subscriptions and seats join that same billing calendar—starting with a prorated charge, then full renewal amounts on every anniversary afterward.

One anniversary per paying party

A company (paying party) keeps a single anchor date for recurring billing across its plans and seats—not a separate renewal date for every line item.

Initial prorated charge

When they buy mid-cycle—first plan, more seats, or an add-on—the first invoice covers only the days until the next anniversary. Amounts are prorated fairly for the partial period.

Full charges on anniversary

After alignment, each renewal (monthly or annual per plan) bills the full subscription amount on the paying party’s anniversary date. 

Day 0 — Paying party established; anniversary date set (e.g. 15th each month)
Day 40 — New seats purchased → prorated invoice for days 40 → next 15th
Each 15th thereafter — Full recurring charge on anniversary (monthly or annual plans)
Cancel mid-period — No refund for unused days; access per merchant policy until period ends

Cancellations

Cancelling a subscription or seat does not refund the unused portion of the current billing period. Customers keep access according to your policy (for example through the paid period end); 2key does not issue automatic credits for remaining days before the anniversary.

Why it matters

Finance teams get one renewal calendar per account. Engineering sees predictable anniversary charges after onboarding. See also Subscription lifecycle and Taxes for prorations on invoices.

Client SDKs

SDKs for major client frameworks—starting with Flutter—deliver license keys and activation data to client apps, online or offline.

Connect client apps to 2key billing and synchronize subscription details while online so entitlements stay current.

Offline sync via signed JWT

Integration

Connect your product to 2key.app billing so entitlements and subscriptions stay authoritative on the platform while your software enforces access.

Hosted SaaS

If you deliver a hosted SaaS product, your backend can query 2key.app billing for subscription data—seats, plans, paying and using parties, and renewal state—so your app gates features and usage from a single source of truth.

Client apps

If you ship an app (mobile, desktop, or embedded), the app can query the billing server directly or through client SDKs. Use online sync when connected, or signed JWT offline sync when users work without network access.

Portals

Merchants run their business in the admin portal; customers and using parties self-serve through storefronts and account areas.

Storefront and checkout

Shoppers browse your category tree, product pages, offerings, and monthly or annual plans—then pay through your connected Stripe or PayPal on merchant.t.2key.app or your CNAME. See Hosting.

Customer self-service

Paying parties view invoices and subscriptions. Using parties obtain license keys or JWTs from the web portal for app activation.

Merchant admin

The admin portal is your control center: build the catalog buyers see, set prices and billing intervals, connect payments, and run day-to-day subscription operations.

Catalog and pricing

  • Create a hierarchical category tree (nested categories for navigation and SEO).
  • Add products and assign them to one or more categories.
  • Define offerings per product (e.g. tiers, bundles, or editions).
  • Add plans under each offering with price—monthly, annual, or other anniversary-aligned intervals.
  • Choose tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive catalog pricing.
  • Preview how category and product pages will appear on your storefront.

Tax, payments, and tenant

  • Set tax rules and registration IDs by jurisdiction.
  • Connect your Stripe and PayPal accounts for customer checkout.
  • Set default currency and tenant URL (subdomain or custom domain).
  • Adjust storefront and checkout branding where supported.

Customers and seats

  • View paying parties (who subscribes) and using parties (who uses the product).
  • Manage subscriptions, renewal dates, and plan changes.
  • Purchase and assign seats; reassign seats as teams change.

Billing operations

  • Review invoices, payments, and credit notes.
  • Track revenue and subscription activity across your tenant.
  • Support upsell, cross-sell, and prorated plan changes—see Subscription lifecycle.

Multi-tenant hosting

Hosted SaaS on PostgreSQL and Node.js. Each request is routed by the Host header to the correct merchant tenant.

Default tenant URL

Merchants are served on tenant subdomains such as merchant.t.2key.app with isolated catalog, checkout, and configuration.

acme.t.2key.app

Custom domain (CNAME)

Point your own hostname at 2key—e.g. billing.yourcompany.com—and serve checkout on your brand’s domain.

billing.merchant.com

Security and trust

Billing data deserves isolation, clear payment boundaries, and sensible operational practices—without overclaiming certifications we do not hold yet.

Tenant isolation

Every HTTP request is routed by the Host header to a single merchant tenant. Catalog, customers, and configuration do not leak across tenants.

Payment security

Card and PayPal checkout run on your connected Stripe and PayPal accounts. Sensitive payment credentials stay with the gateways; 2key receives limited confirmation metadata.

Data protection

HTTPS for the marketing site and tenant endpoints. Reasonable technical and organizational measures; details in our Privacy Policy.

Your responsibilities

Secure your applications, API keys, and JWT distribution. End-customer refunds and chargebacks are handled between you and your customers per our Terms.

FAQ

Common questions from SaaS and app vendors evaluating 2key.app billing.

Who is the merchant of record to my customers?

You are. Your customers buy from you on your storefront; 2key.app provides hosted billing infrastructure. You connect your own Stripe or PayPal for customer payments.

Can I use my own Stripe account?

Yes. Merchants connect their own Stripe (and PayPal) for charging end customers. The US$25 setup fee and platform fees (1% / $25 minimum) are paid to 2key.app separately—see Pricing.

How does offline activation work?

Using parties sign in to your merchant web portal and receive a signed JWT with their entitlements. They paste it into your app; the SDK validates the signature locally. See SDKs.

Can I use my own domain for checkout?

Yes. CNAME a host such as billing.yourcompany.com to your 2key tenant. See Multi-tenant hosting.

How does proration work when a customer buys mid-cycle?

Each paying party has one anniversary date. New plans or seats get an initial prorated charge through the next anniversary, then full recurring amounts on that date. See Onboarding and anniversary alignment.

Do cancellations refund unused time?

No. Cancelling does not refund the unused portion of the current period. Access follows your cancellation policy (for example until the paid period ends).

Do you support free trials?

Trial and freemium models are on our roadmap. Contact hello@2key.app to discuss your use case during early access.

What about failed payments and dunning?

Automated retry and dunning workflows are planned. Today, subscription state and invoicing are managed on the platform; contact us for current capabilities.

Is there an API or webhooks?

Hosted SaaS products can query billing for subscription data. Server-side webhooks for events are planned—see Integration and reach out for early API access.
