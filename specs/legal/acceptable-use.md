This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) applies to all users of 2key.app, including Merchants and anyone accessing merchant storefronts or APIs. It supplements our [Terms of Service](terms.html).

## You may not use the Service to

- Violate any applicable law, regulation, or third-party rights.
- Sell or promote illegal goods, services, or content.
- Process payments for fraud, money laundering, or sanctioned parties.
- Distribute malware, phishing, or deceptive software licensing schemes.
- Infringe intellectual property or distribute pirated software license keys.
- Harass, threaten, or discriminate against others.
- Send unsolicited bulk messages (spam) using data obtained through the Service.
- Probe, scan, or test vulnerabilities without written authorization.
- Overload or disrupt the Service (e.g. denial-of-service, abusive API volume).
- Circumvent access controls, seat limits, or billing mechanisms.
- Misrepresent your identity, business, or products offered to customers.

## Merchant responsibilities

- Ensure your products and license terms are accurately described.
- Honor refunds and customer support obligations required by law and your policies.
- Configure tax and compliance settings appropriate to your jurisdictions.
- Use payment gateways in line with Stripe, PayPal, and card network rules.

## Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and remove content, suspend tenants, or terminate accounts without prior notice where necessary to protect users, partners, or the platform. We may report illegal activity to authorities.

## Reporting abuse

To report a violation, contact [hello@2key.app](mailto:hello@2key.app) with relevant URLs, merchant name, and a description of the issue.

## Changes

We may update this AUP; continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.
